**Build — NEW project: Personal Blog / Link Hub**
Build a simple **personal site** in Next.js with at least two pages: a home page with a bio and links, and a blog page that renders a list of posts from hardcoded data. The focus is on routing (multiple pages), layouts (shared nav bar), and deploying to Vercel.

This is a **separate, new project** from your Week 1–2 Task Tracker. You're not extending that one — Next.js has a different project structure and you want fresh muscle memory with it. You *will* keep building on this blog project from Week 5 onward though, so pick a topic you don't mind spending a few weeks with.

> **Why this project?** A personal site is low-stakes, immediately useful, and scaffolds naturally into a full-featured app as you add layers in coming weeks.

**Milestone:** A live website at a real Vercel URL (e.g. `yourname.vercel.app`) with multiple pages and a working nav bar.
